## 1. Getting started

### Create an account
1. Open **https://lukulu-bread-of-life.web.app**.
2. Tap **Need an account? Sign up**.
3. Enter your **full name**, **email** and a **password** (at least 6 characters), then tap **Sign Up**.

Every new account starts as a **Member**. To get a different role (Finance,
Secretariat, Elder and so on), sign up first, then ask an **Admin** to assign
your role (see [Account Roles](#10-account-roles-admin)). Nobody can give
themselves a higher role.

### Sign in
Enter your email and password and tap **Login** (pressing **Enter** on the
password box also works).

### Forgot your password
1. On the login page tap **Forgot password?**
2. Confirm your email and tap **Send link**.
3. Open the email and follow the link to choose a new password.

For privacy the app always says "if an account exists..." whether or not the
address is registered.

### Sign out
Open your profile (bottom of the left sidebar on a computer, or the round
initial at the top right of Home on a phone) and choose **Logout**.

## 2. Roles: who can do what

A person can hold **more than one role** (for example Finance and Elder); they
get everything either role allows.

| Role | Members directory | Giving and finances | Attendance | Events | Announcements | Prayer requests |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| **Member** | - | Own giving only | - | View | Read (public) | Own requests (edit, delete) |
| **Elder / Deacon / Deaconess** | - | - | Record | View | Post and read | See all |
| **Pastor** | Manage | **View only** | Record | Manage | Post and read | See all |
| **Secretariat** | Manage | - | Record | Manage | Post and read | See all |
| **Hospitality** | Manage | - | - | View | Read (public) | Own requests |
| **Finance Officer** | - | Manage | - | View | Read (public) | Own requests |
| **Financial Oversight** | - | **View only** | - | View | Read (public) | Own requests |
| **Admin** | Manage | Manage | Record | Manage | Post and read | See all |

Notes:
- **Financial records are confidential.** Only Admin and Finance can enter or change
  them. The Pastor and Financial Oversight can read them but cannot change anything.
  Elders, Deacons and Deaconesses do **not** see finances unless they also hold
  Finance or Oversight.
- These rules are enforced by the server, not just hidden in the screens.

## 3. Finding your way around

- **On a computer or tablet (wide screen):** a sidebar on the left lists the
  sections available to you. Your name and role are shown at the bottom.
- **On a phone:** a bar at the bottom shows your main sections; tap **More** for
  the rest.
- Sections you are not allowed to use are simply not shown.

## 4. Home (dashboard)

Home greets you by name and shows:

- **Banner:** the next upcoming event, or a welcome message if none is scheduled.
  Buttons: **Watch Live** (opens the church Facebook page), **View Events**,
  **Join WhatsApp**.
- **Upcoming Events:** the next three events (tap **View all** for the full list).
- **Quick Access:** shortcut tiles to the sections you use most.
- **Daily Verse:** a new Bible verse each day.
- **Overview:** live counts (members, last attendance, this month's giving if you
  may see it, announcements, prayer requests).

## 5. Giving and finances

*Available to: Finance Officer, Admin (full access); Financial Oversight (view only).*

Open **Giving** in the sidebar. You will see **This Month** and **All Time**
totals (Kwacha) and the list of recorded contributions.

**Toolbar icons (top right):**

| Icon | Name | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Summary sheet | **Weekly Statement** | One Sunday's figures: giving by category, bank deposit and expenses, denomination breakdown |
| Download arrow | **Export data (CSV)** | Download giving and forms as spreadsheets |
| Chart | **Monthly & Yearly Summary** | Totals for a month or a year, printable |
| Receipt | **Sunday Income Forms** | All saved weekly forms, and the Activity log |

**The blue + button** offers:

- **Record Contribution** - a single named gift. Fill in **Member Name**,
  **Amount (ZMW)** and **Category** (Tithe, Offering, Seed, Thanksgiving or
  Pledge). Optionally use **Link to member account** to attach the gift to a
  registered member so it appears in that member's **My Giving**.
- **Sunday Income Form** - the full weekly count (next section).

> Deleting a single giving record asks you to confirm first.

## 6. The Sunday Income Form

This is the digital version of the paper "Income Analysis Breakdown (Form 2)",
plus the NATSAVE bank-deposit sheet. **Fill it in once** - the app then creates
the giving records and the attendance record for you.

### Open it
**Giving -> + -> Sunday Income Form.**

### Step by step

1. **Service Sunday and service.** Pick the Sunday (only Sundays can be chosen)
   and **Morning** or **Evening**.
2. **Count the cash by category.** There is one table each for **Tithe**,
   **Offering**, **Seed**, **Thanksgiving** and **Pledge**. Type *how many* of
   each note or coin you counted (K500, K200, K100, K50, K20, K10, K5, K2, K1,
   50 ngwee, 10 ngwee, 5 ngwee). The sub-total updates as you type. Leave a
   line blank for zero.
3. **Forex.** If foreign currency was given, choose the currency and type the
   amount; it is converted to Kwacha automatically. Use **Add row** for more.
4. **Cheques.** Name, cheque number and amount. **Add row** for more.
5. **Natsave Account Deposit.** Count the cash being banked in the church's
   NATSAVE account (all income except the tithe of tithes and expenses).
6. **Expenses + Tithe of Tithes Reserve.** Count the cash held back locally to
   cover expenses and the tithe-of-tithes remittance.
7. **Expenses.** List each expense (for example *Missions*, *Tissue*,
   *Alms giving*) with its amount. **Add row** for more.
8. **Check the reconciliation message.** The reserve minus the listed expenses
   should equal **20% of the tithe**. If it does not, a gold warning shows the
   difference. You can still save - discrepancies must be recorded, not hidden -
   but check your counts first.
9. **Attendance.** Enter **Men**, **Women** and **Children**.
10. **Sign-off.** Fill in **Prepared By** (pre-filled with your name),
    **Checked By** and **Collected By**. Under each name there is a box where the
    person can **draw their signature** with a finger or mouse; tap **Clear** to
    redo it. Signatures are optional. (While you draw, the page will not scroll.)
11. **Totals (live).** The card above the Save button shows cash, forex, cheques,
    **Grand Total**, 20% of tithe, Natsave deposit, total collection and total
    expenses as you work.
12. Tap **Save Form**.

### After saving
- A detail page shows every figure, the expenses list and the signatures.
- The app has created one **giving record per category** (with a cash count) and
  one **attendance record** for that Sunday.
- An entry is added to the **Activity log** (see below).

### Editing a form
Open the form (**Giving -> Sunday Income Forms -> tap the form**) and tap the
**pencil**. The form opens filled in; change what you need and **Save Form**. The
giving and attendance records are updated to match, and a saved signature is kept
unless you draw a new one.

### Deleting a form
Tap the **bin** on the form and confirm. This also removes the giving and
attendance records that form created. **This cannot be undone**, though the
deletion is permanently listed in the Activity log.

> **Who can edit or delete:** Finance and Admin only. Financial Oversight can open
> and read forms but sees no edit or delete buttons.
>
> **Older forms:** forms saved before editing was introduced show **Read-only**.
> They cannot be changed from the app; ask the developer if one needs correcting.

### Activity log
**Giving -> Sunday Income Forms -> history icon.** A permanent, newest-first list of
who **created, edited or deleted** a form, which form, the total before and after,
and when. Entries cannot be changed or removed by anyone.

## 7. Reports, printing and backups

### Print or download a form
On a form's detail page:
- **Download PDF** (arrow) saves a dated file such as
  `income-form-2026-10-04-morning.pdf`.
- **Print** (printer) opens your browser's print window. If the browser cannot
  open it, the app tells you and downloads the PDF instead - open the file and
  print it from there.

The PDF is a clean, paginated copy of the paper form with the Natsave, expenses,
attendance and signature sections, suitable for filing at the Pastor's office.

### Weekly Statement
**Giving -> Weekly Statement.** Tap the date to choose a Sunday. It shows giving
by category, the grand total, a **Bank Deposit & Expenses** section (tithe of
tithes, Natsave, total collection, expenses reserve, total expenses, with a
warning if a form that week does not reconcile) and the cash denomination breakdown.

### Monthly and Yearly Summary
**Giving -> Monthly & Yearly Summary.**
1. Choose **Month** or **Year**.
2. Use the arrows to move to earlier periods (you cannot go past the present).
3. Read giving by category, bank deposit and expenses, attendance (services,
   total, average) and giving week by week.
4. Use **Download PDF** or **Print** to produce a filing copy.

### Export to a spreadsheet (backup)
**Giving -> Export data.**
1. Choose a year, or **All years**.
2. Tap **CSV** beside **Giving records** or **Sunday Income Forms**.
3. A file such as `giving-2026.csv` or `income-forms-all.csv` downloads. Open it
   in Excel or Google Sheets.

Keep a copy somewhere safe (for example a drive that only Finance can access) -
it contains confidential financial information.

## 8. My Giving (every member)

**My Giving** shows only contributions that Finance has linked to **your** account.
Nobody else can see them.

- **Tithes only** filters to tithes; **All dates** lets you pick a date range.
- Totals are shown separately for each currency.
- Each entry shows a **Reference**. If something is missing or wrong, contact the
  Finance team and quote the reference. Older gifts may need to be linked by Finance.

### Yearly statement
At the top of My Giving, the **Yearly statement** card lets you pick a year and
**Download PDF** or **Print** a personal statement: totals by category and month and
an itemised list. It lists only gifts recorded against your account, and says so.

## 9. Members, attendance, events, announcements, prayer

**Members** (Secretariat, Hospitality, Admin). The directory of the congregation.
Tap **+** to add a member (full name, email, phone); use the **pencil** beside a
name to edit and the **bin** to delete. Personal contact details are only visible
to these roles.

**Attendance** (Elders, Deacons, Deaconesses, Pastor, Secretariat, Admin). Tap **+**
for **Add Attendance Record**: enter **Men**, **Women**, **Children** and **New
Visitors**. (Attendance also arrives automatically from every Sunday Income Form.)

**Events** (everyone views; Secretariat and Admin manage). Add an event with a
title, date and location. Upcoming events also appear on Home.

**Announcements.** Leaders (Elders, Deacons, Deaconesses, Pastor, Secretariat,
Admin) can post. An announcement can be for **everyone** or for **leaders only**;
leaders-only posts are hidden from ordinary members.

**Prayer.** Anyone can submit a prayer request: tap **+** and write in the large
box (up to 1000 characters). You see your own requests; church leaders see all of
them so they can pray and respond. On a request **you** posted, tap the **three
dots** to **Edit** the wording, **Mark as answered** (or back to pending), or
**Delete** it. Leaders can mark or delete anyone's request but cannot reword it.

**WhatsApp and Facebook.** **Join WhatsApp** on Home opens the branch community
group. **Watch Live** opens the church Facebook page for live services.

## 10. Account Roles (Admin)

*Admin only.* **Account Roles** lists registered accounts.

1. Find the person in the list (they must already have signed up).
2. Tap their name. A **Roles for ...** box opens with a tick-box for each role.
3. Tick one or more roles and save. Their access updates the next time their screen loads.

### Add a staff account

To set up a pastor, finance officer or leader who has not signed up yet, tap
**Add staff** on the Account Roles screen. Enter their **name** and **email**, tick
their roles and tap **Create account**. You never see or set a password: they are
emailed a link to choose their own, then sign in as normal. If the email is already
registered, change their role from the list instead.

### The very first Admin

The first Admin cannot be made from inside the app. Sign up as normal, then in the
Firebase console open **Firestore Database > users**, open your own document and set
`roles` to the list `["admin"]`. Sign out and in again; from then on use Account
Roles and Add staff.

Safeguards: an Admin cannot remove their own Admin role (to avoid locking everyone
out), and only an Admin can change roles.

## 11. Troubleshooting and FAQ

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| I cannot see **Giving** | Only Finance, Admin and Oversight see it. Ask an Admin to check your role. Members see **My Giving**. |
| A screen says "permission" or nothing loads | Your role may not allow it. Ask an Admin. Try signing out and in once. |
| The page looks old after an update | Refresh the browser (Ctrl+F5, or clear the site's cache). |
| **Print** does nothing | Use **Download PDF** and print the file. |
| I cannot edit a form | Only Finance and Admin can, and only forms saved after editing was introduced. |
| The reconciliation warning will not go away | Reserve minus expenses must equal 20% of the tithe. Recount the reserve or add any missing expense. You may still save if the difference is genuine. |
| A signature will not draw | Draw inside the white box. On a phone, keep your finger on the box until the stroke ends. |
| I forgot my password | Use **Forgot password?** on the login page. |
| I deleted a form by mistake | It cannot be restored from the app. The Activity log shows what it contained (date, service, total); re-enter it. Export backups help. |

## 12. Good practice for financial records

1. **Two people count.** Prepared By, Checked By and Collected By should be three
   people (or at least two) who each take responsibility.
2. **Enter the form the same day** while counts are fresh.
3. **Resolve warnings before filing.** Investigate any reconciliation difference.
4. **Print or download a PDF** for the Pastor's file after each service.
5. **Export a backup monthly** and store it safely.
6. **Review the Activity log** regularly for unexpected edits or deletions.
7. **Protect your password.** Do not share it; sign out on shared devices.
